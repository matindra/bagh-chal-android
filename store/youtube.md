## Description (max 5000)
Bagh Chal ("move the tiger") is the traditional strategy board game of Nepal and India, now on Android. Four tigers hunt twenty goats on a 5×5 board. It's quick to learn and hard to master.

▶ Download free on Google Play:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.matinstudios.baghchal

HOW TO PLAY
🐯 Tigers hunt: jump over a goat to capture it. Catch 5 goats and the tigers win.
🐐 Goats trap: work together and box in every tiger so none can move.
Goats go first, placing one goat at a time until all twenty are on the board.

PLAY YOUR WAY
