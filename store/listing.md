## Full description (max 4000)
Bagh Chal ("move the tiger") is the traditional strategy board game from Nepal. Four tigers hunt twenty goats on a 5×5 board. It is quick to learn and hard to master.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. They jump over a goat in a straight line to the empty point beyond.
• Goats win by trapping all four tigers so none can move.
• Goats move first. They are placed on the board one by one, then they move.
• Every game is a different puzzle: tigers attack and goats build walls.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Player vs AI: play as Tigers or Goats against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard.
• Play Online: start a quick match, or create a private game and share a 4-digit code with a friend.
• Player vs Player: two players take turns on one phone or tablet.

MADE TO BE EASY TO PLAY
• Tap a piece to see all its legal moves and captures.
• Smooth animations, sound effects and vibration (you can turn each one off).
• Undo and Hint for when you're stuck against the AI.
• Your game is saved automatically, so you can carry on later.
• A How to Play guide with pictures teaches the rules in a minute.
• Stats show your wins, losses and draws as Tigers and as Goats.
• Light and dark themes, plus Wood, Stone and Cloth boards.
• Play against the AI with no internet connection.

FREE TO PLAY
Bagh Chal is free with ads. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off banners and ads between games, makes Undo and Hint free and unlocks every board.

Can you trap the tigers, or will the tigers break through? Download Bagh Chal and find out.
